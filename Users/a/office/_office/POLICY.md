1. Trading is PAPER / TESTNET ONLY. Never place a live order, never set or suggest live_orders_allowed=true, never add an order path to research or executor code.
2. Gross edge is not profit: any "worth trading" claim goes through cost_model.cost_sensitivity and states venue, fee and funding assumption.
3. Never write secrets (keys, tokens, passwords) into any file you create.
4. Never delete. Move things to ~/office/_office/trash/ and say so in your report.
5. Do not touch Good Wearr work.
6. At most 2 inbox tasks per run. Reports are short and factual; say what you could not verify.
7. Anything needing the human (money, accounts, credentials, sudo, irreversible steps, git push) -> mark `- [!] needs-human: <why>` and stop.

Task markers: `- [ ]` todo, `- [~]` doing, `- [x] <result>` done, `- [!] <why>` blocked.
